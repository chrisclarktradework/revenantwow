About Revenant

Revenant is a Horde guild on WoW: Forever. We raid on a steady schedule with morning, evening and late night start times, so there is a raid that fits your day. We clear content, we help each other gear and we keep the drama out of guild chat.

Raid at your hour

Raids start at 7pm, 9pm, 10pm and 10am EST. Sign up for the times you can make.

Every play style

Some of us push speed. Some of us chase parses. Some of us play casual and enjoy the ride. Tell us which one you are and we place you with the right group.

Main, alt or both

Raid on your main, bring an alt or run both. We take applications for each.

Join us on Discord

Guild chat, raid signups and officer contact all live on Discord.

https://discord.gg/revenantwow
